How to read this table Symbols and percentile rules

Symbols and filters

A dash means a journal is in the selected universe but has no score. Zero is shown as a number. Journals outside the selected universe are listed last, with a link to a universe that contains them.

Search, filters, sorting and pages only change what you see. They never change the percentiles.

What the scores measure, the journal universes and Raw and Filtered scores are explained on the About page.

How percentiles are calculated

Start with all journals in the selected universe with a defined score, and apply the reference-coverage and publication-history requirements. Within each field, percentile = 100 × number of eligible journals with an equal or lower score ÷ number of eligible journals in that field. Journals without a field form an explicit “Unclassified” group.

Keep journals whose field percentile is strictly above 100 minus the retained top percentage. Ties receive their highest shared percentile, so the retained share can exceed the requested percentage. The pool percentile then uses the same formula across all retained journals, on a 0–100 scale.

The example preset uses Filtered scores in the Norwegian Register universe, ANS, OpenAlex fields, reference coverage strictly above 20%, output in at least 4 of 5 years, and the top 70% within each field.
